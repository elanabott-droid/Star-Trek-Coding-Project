# Star-Trek-Coding-Project
Star Trek: Through the Ages is aimed at bringing the theme song from the 1966 Star Trek series to life in a way that accurately reflects the original song while still being viable as a coded piece of music. This was done to pay homage to the series that brought so many joy when they were young, while also giving a sense of modernity. The project consists of 6 piano cells, each with their own parts that allign. These include the Right Hand, the Right Hand Extra, the Left hand, the Left hand Extra, the Supporting Chords and the Supporting Chords Extra.
